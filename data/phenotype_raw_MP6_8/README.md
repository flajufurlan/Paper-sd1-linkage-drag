Dataset: Raw phenotypic data for trials MP6/8 Population
Columns:
•	Year: Year of experiment
•	Location: Field location of trial
•	Genotype: Germplasm identifier
•	Replicate: Replication number
•	Block: Block number (RCBD or augmented design)
•	Plot: Plot identifier
•	Row, Column: Spatial coordinates of field layout
•	Plant_height_cm: Plant height measured in centimeters
•	Yield_lb_ac: Grain yield measured in pounds per acre
Notes:
•	Data include multiple environments (years and locations)
•	Both RCBD and augmented designs were used
•	Spatial coordinates (Row, Column) are included for reproducibility and spatial analysis
